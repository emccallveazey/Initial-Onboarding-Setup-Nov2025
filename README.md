# <span style="color:#004b8d;">Initial Onboarding Setup – One-Page Guide</span>

**Average timeline:** ~8 business days from quote acceptance to service readiness.  
**Key contacts:** Project Installer (primary coordinator) · Account Executive (escalation partner)

---

## <span style="color:#004b8d;">Timeline</span>

| Day | Milestone | What Happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Quote Acceptance | Customer approves quote and workflow is uploaded to internal systems. |
| Day 1 | Workflow Creation | Workflow is generated, assigned, and an introduction email is sent with the Project Installer and Account Executive copied. |
| Days 2–3 | Account Build | ATN is ordered, assigned, and tested; domain setup begins; E911 services are configured; Project Installer reaches out to start kickoff conversation. |
| Days 4–5 | Initial Routing Setup | Seat-based extensions (e.g., 200–202) are created; ATN forwards through routing user to extension 200 voicemail. |
| Days 6–8 | Training & Service Availability | Project Installer confirms next steps and shares training resources for Portal, Clarity App, Texting, and Call Recording. |

---

## <span style="color:#004b8d;">Action Checklist</span>

- Upload and assign workflows immediately after quote acceptance.
- Track ATN procurement, domain setup, and E911 configuration through completion.
- Document extension builds and routing handoffs for the customer record.
- Confirm customer contacts for the kickoff conversation and align on go-live expectations.
- Share the Clarity Voice training library and highlight the most relevant sessions.

---

## <span style="color:#004b8d;">Training Resources</span>

- Portal, Clarity App, Texting, and Call Recording videos: <a href="https://support.clarityvoice.com/portal/en/kb/products/additional-services" style="color:#f05a28;">Clarity Voice Knowledge Base</a>
- Encourage the customer to review videos ahead of training to accelerate adoption.

---

**Ready to launch?** Complete the checklist and schedule the final training session with the Project Installer.
