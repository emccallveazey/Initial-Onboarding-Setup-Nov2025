# <span style="color:#004b8d;">Initial Onboarding Setup – One-Pager</span>

> <span style="color:#f05a28;">Goal:</span> Activate new Clarity Voice customers within eight business days of quote acceptance through a consistent, trackable workflow.

## <span style="color:#004b8d;">Timeline & Ownership</span>

| Day | Milestone | Team Actions | Customer Touchpoints |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Quote Acceptance | Upload the signed workflow into internal systems. | Confirm receipt of acceptance (automated or manual). |
| Day 1 | Workflow Creation | Generate the workflow, assign Project Installer, notify Account Executive. | Send introduction email copying the Project Installer and Account Executive. |
| Days 2–3 | Account Build | Order, assign, and test ATN; launch domain setup; configure E911. | Project Installer reaches out (call/text/email) to schedule kickoff. |
| Days 4–5 | Initial Routing Setup | Build seat-based extensions (e.g., 200–202); map ATN to routing user and extension 200 voicemail. | Provide customer with initial extension list and confirm routing preferences. |
| Days 6–8 | Training & Launch Prep | Verify core features, finalize documentation, confirm go-live date. | Share portal, Clarity App, texting, and call recording training resources; align on service start. |

## <span style="color:#004b8d;">Execution Checklist</span>

- [ ] Workflow uploaded and owner assigned on Day 0.
- [ ] ATN ordered, activated, and tested before Day 3 concludes.
- [ ] Domain setup and E911 configuration documented with timestamps.
- [ ] Extension map, routing logic, and voicemail destinations confirmed with customer.
- [ ] Kickoff conversation completed and expectations for go-live captured in CRM notes.
- [ ] Training materials sent with follow-up date scheduled for Day 6–8.

## <span style="color:#004b8d;">Resources & References</span>

- Training videos: <a href="https://support.clarityvoice.com/portal/en/kb/products/additional-services" style="color:#f05a28;">Clarity Voice Knowledge Base</a>
- Support escalation: Coordinate with Account Executive for any blockers impacting the Day 8 launch target.
- Documentation: Store all artifacts (workflow, routing plans, kickoff notes) in the customer's project folder.
